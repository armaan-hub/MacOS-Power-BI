# Task 174 — dax-hunt-174: BETA.DIST is refused where Power BI overflows or the app's tail values drift

Goal: the t231 fresh batch (1443 answered rows, none asked before) left 6 wrong. Five are known last-digit limits (COS, SIN, BETA.INV, NORM.DIST and T.DIST.RT tails). The sixth is `BETA.DIST(0.05,SUM(Facts[Sales]),1E15,TRUE())`: Power BI raises "The result of function 'BETA.DIST' is too large (overflow)", and the app answered 1.

Spec: t232, 117 BETA.DIST probes measured in Power BI Desktop. None of it is read by the app. The measurements show three things:
- **The overflow band is irregular** and widens with the large shape.
  - At 1E15, shapes from 24.2 to 35 error, while 24 and 40 to 2958465 answer.
  - At 1E20, 30, 50 and 100 error. At 1E100, 30 and 100 error.
  - Mirrored, (1E15,25), (1E13,30) and (1E20,50) error, while (1E15,40), (1E15,60) and (1E15,100) answer.
- **Tiny tails drift.** With a shape of 1E6 or more, the app's cumulative tails below about 1E-8 differ by 1-3%. For example, (1E-15,21,1E15) is 7.54E-21 in Power BI and 7.42E-21 in the app.
- **No simple rule** separates the error and answer cases, so the app refuses rather than models them.
