# Task 150 — dax-hunt-150: CURRENCY amount rule, refined and extended (batch 8)

Goal: remove the wrong answers found by 780 fresh random probes (13 groups, seeds 5001+) and targeted CURRENCY probes
of financial amount arguments, checked against Power BI Desktop in the guest. Refusing is always better than guessing.

Spec: 869 measured expressions (`spec`) plus a hidden `holdout` (6) and 52 review probes. None is read by the app.

Families (Haiku 5.5 coded, Sonnet 5.5 reviewed and tested in the real app):
- A. A financial result is CURRENCY only when at least one amount argument is CURRENCY and every other amount argument
  is statically CURRENCY, whole number or BLANK; any double-typed amount gives the plain double
  (PMT(0.05,2,CURRENCY(1.23456),2.0) = -1.63958365853659, ...,INT(2.5)) = -1.6396). Column reads inside ADDCOLUMNS/ROW
  carry their column type; ISO.CEILING with its default significance is a whole number.
- B. More CURRENCY amount positions: PRICE and ODDFPRICE/ODDLPRICE redemption, PRICEDISC redemption, RECEIVED investment,
  ACCRINT/ACCRINTM par, DOLLARDE/DOLLARFR value. AMORLINC, PRICEMAT, TBILLPRICE, YIELD and rate positions stay double.
- C. DIVIDE of a CURRENCY by a number and CONVERT(..., CURRENCY) are CURRENCY in the MEDIANX midpoint rule.

Narrowed: CONVERT as CURRENCY only for the MEDIANX midpoint (applying it everywhere broke a saved VARX.P answer).
Left (backlog): PMT/PRICE double results differing in the 15th digit (also wrong before this task).
