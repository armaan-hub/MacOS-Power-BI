Planned by Claude Opus 5.5 (Team-Planner), coded by Haiku 5.5 (Antigravity was out of quota), reviewed and tested by Sonnet 5.5 (Team-Thinker), two rounds.

- Supported types come from one constant, `_CONVERT_TYPE_OPTIONS`, which also fills the convert_type editor. The menus no longer switch the live editor to read the supported types.
