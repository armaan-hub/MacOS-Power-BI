# Task 193: value-axis labels on charts, like Power BI

Planned by Claude Opus 5.5 (Team-Planner), coded by Antigravity, fixed, reviewed and tested by Sonnet 5.5 (Team-Thinker).

- Column, line, area and line-and-clustered-column charts draw a left **value axis**:
  - "nice" ticks starting at 0, with a step of 1, 2, 2.5 or 5 × 10^n (`niceTicks`);
  - labels in Auto display units: as-is below 1,000, then K, M, bn, T (`axisValueLabel`);
  - gridlines on the ticks.

  Bar charts draw the value axis along the bottom.
- Examples: 0..37 gives 0, 10, 20, 30, 40; 0..1234 gives 0..1250 in steps of 250; 2500 is "2.5K"; 4.2e9 is "4.2bn".
- Negative values: the ticks go below 0, and bars and points are drawn from the zero line (`zeroFrac`). This was a Sonnet fix.
- The Format pane's Y-axis switch (column, line, area, lineClusteredColumn) and X-axis switch (bar, line) now work.
- Other chart types keep their old padding; this was a Sonnet fix.
- The tick and unit rules are the Planner's knowledge and are not yet checked on the VM.
- Tests:
  - tests/test_ux193_value_axis.py;
  - the axis-card table in tests/test_ux189_format_pane.py, an authorised edit.
