# Task 146 — dax-hunt-146: wrong DAX answers from a fresh 14-group hunt (batch 4)

Goal: remove the wrong answers found by 682 fresh random probes (14 groups, seeds 4601+), 76 targeted probes and a
trig argument ladder, checked against Power BI Desktop in the guest. Refusing is always better than guessing.

Spec: 781 measured expressions (`spec`) plus a hidden `holdout` (24). None is read by the app.

Families (Haiku 5.5 coded, Sonnet 5.5 reviewed and tested in the real app):
- B. NETWORKDAYS with a weekend code outside 1..7 and 11..17 (0, 8, 10, 18, -1, BLANK()) is a query-level error in
  Power BI that IFERROR does not catch; the app now refuses it the same way (IFERROR no longer returns its fallback).
  Fractional codes round half-up (1.5 -> 44, 11.5 -> 52 over Jan-Feb 2024); text codes keep their locked answers.

Tried and reverted because they broke locked Power BI answers (moved to the hunt backlog for a follow-up task):
- A. A whole number plus a double literal (1E15) is a double in Power BI, shown with 15 significant digits
  (123456789012345+1E15 = 1.12345678901235E+15); refusing it broke 15 locked answers that use the sum inside MIN, MAX,
  ISNUMBER, IFERROR and ROUND. Needs a display-path change.
- C. Refusing SIN/COS/TAN/COT above |x| = 200 contradicts thousands of locked trig answers (e.g. COS(45000.5)&"z").
  Large-argument trig stays a known precision limit.
- D. LN/LOG/LOG10 as the binary double (LN(6) = 1.79175946922805) broke locked (EXP(LN(3))/3-1)&"z" rows.
