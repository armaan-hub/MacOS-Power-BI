# Task 148 — dax-hunt-148: wrong DAX answers from a fresh 13-group hunt (batch 6)

Goal: remove the wrong answers found by 780 fresh random probes (13 groups, seeds 4801+) and targeted probes of
CURRENCY financial results, trig near multiples of pi/4 and date sets over computed columns, checked against Power BI
Desktop in the guest. Refusing is always better than guessing.

Spec: 981 measured expressions (`spec`) plus a hidden `holdout` (10). None is read by the app.

Families (Haiku 5.5 coded, Sonnet 5.5 reviewed and tested in the real app):
- A. PMT, IPMT, PPMT, FV, PV, CUMIPMT, CUMPRINC, SLN, SYD, DB, DDB, VDB and ISPMT return CURRENCY when an amount
  argument (pv, fv, pmt, cost, salvage) is CURRENCY: n = round half away from zero of (double result * 10000) computed
  in double, shown with 4 places (PMT(0.05,2,CURRENCY(1.23456)) = -0.664; PMT(0.05,2,CURRENCY(-922337203685477)) =
  496037447347921.1008). Rate, nper, life, type and factor do not. A result outside the Currency range raises
  Power BI's overflow error, which IFERROR catches.
- B. SIN/COS/TAN at an exact double multiple of pi/4 follow Power BI's octant values (SIN(PI()) = -0.707106781186548,
  COS(PI()/2) = 0, TAN(PI()/4) is a division-by-zero error); arguments within 2 ulps but not exact are refused. Checked
  on 130 fresh probes (k*PI()/4, RADIANS, ASIN/ACOS/ATAN results): all equal to Power BI. COT keeps its existing model.
- C. SIN/COS/TAN with pi/4 < |x| < 10 and a result smaller than |x|*1e-4 are refused (Power BI's reduced-precision
  argument reduction makes those digits differ).
- D. Date-set functions over a column made by SELECTCOLUMNS, ADDCOLUMNS or SUMMARIZE are an error, as for CALENDAR.

Narrowed (refusing more broke saved Power BI answers): refusing exact pi/4 multiples broke 165 saved rows, refusing COT
broke 35, and the brief's tiny-result threshold refused saved large-argument answers.
