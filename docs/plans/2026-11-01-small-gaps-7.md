## Part B: EVALUATEANDLOG around a filter modifier (33)

## Part E: a table on a row (22)
Refusal today: "CALCULATE of a table on a row is not modelled by the local engine."
`MAXX(ADDCOLUMNS(Facts,"q",CALCULATE(VALUES(Facts[Region]),Facts[Region]="East")),[q])` is East. `SUMX(ADDCOLUMNS(Facts,"q",CALCULATE(VALUES(Facts[Note]),Facts[Region]="East")),1)` is 6. A single-row result is a scalar; several rows is an error in Power BI, and the rule must say which rows are in the table.
