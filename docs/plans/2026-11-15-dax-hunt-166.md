# Task 166 — dax-hunt-166: df-2 Student t right and two-tailed tails without cancellation (batch 23)

Goal: fresh probes found that T.DIST.RT and T.DIST.2T with 2 degrees of freedom lost their last digits for large t
(T.DIST.RT(80,2) pbi 7.81066942201931E-05, T.DIST.RT(9E5,2) pbi 6.17283950616141E-13, app 6.17284001691587E-13): the
app computed the tail as a complement of a value close to 1, which cancels.

Spec: 2008 random expressions (t182), a 291-row statistics corpus, a 220-row grid (t183), 120 fresh validation rows
(t185), and two df-2 validation sets (t186: 33 seeded rows, t187: 42 grid rows from t = 2.1 to 70), all measured in
Power BI. None is read by the app.

Work (Haiku 5.5 coded; Sonnet 5.5 headed review and testing):
- analytics_studio/dax_stats.py (`_t_tail_stable`): with df = 2 and t/r > 0.95, where r = sqrt(2+t*t), the right tail
  is 1/((r+t)*r) (two-tailed: twice that). Below 0.95 the existing complement form stays, because Power BI's own answers
  carry its cancellation noise through t = 3.5. The 0.95 threshold is fitted to measured rows (any value in
  (0.9272, 0.954] fits them all); it is not a derived precision limit.
- tests/test_iteration254_hunt_166.py: measured rows; known limits marked expectedFailure.
