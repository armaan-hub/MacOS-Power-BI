# Task 185: Model view drag-to-relate, and editing or deleting a relationship from its line

Planned by Claude Opus 5.5 (Team-Planner). Coded by Antigravity, with two drag follow-ups by Haiku 5.5. Reviewed and tested by Sonnet 5.5 (Team-Thinker) over three rounds, with real QTest drags.

- Dragging a column from one table card onto a column of another opens New relationship with both endpoints prefilled. Cardinality and direction come from the dialog's existing detection. Nothing is saved until OK. A drop on the same table does nothing.
- Double-clicking a relationship line opens Edit relationship. Right-clicking gives Properties and Delete; Delete asks for confirmation.
- Controller: `_run_relationship_dialog` (shared by manage_relationships), createRelationshipFromDrag, editRelationship (an unknown id is refused) and deleteRelationship. The validate-and-save tail is shared.
- QML: the drag proxy follows the cursor and the MouseArea uses preventStealing, so dropping works anywhere on a row, including the right edge. The column ScrollBar shows only when the list overflows. Dragging inside a row no longer flick-scrolls; the wheel and the scrollbar still scroll.
